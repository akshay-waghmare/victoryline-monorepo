**Primary outcome KPI:** Google Search Console discovery and indexing. The technical score below is only a prerequisite for giving Google a clean crawl path; it is not GSC discovery or indexing and must never be reported as either.

**90% operating target (measured, not guaranteed):**

- **Technical discoverability gate:** at least 90% of sampled upcoming URLs must return 200, self-canonical, `index,follow`, match-specific title/H1 and scheduled context, valid `SportsEvent`, sitemap membership, and at least one server-rendered link from the home/matches/live-score/today/schedule hub set.
- **Google discovery gate (primary):** at least 90% of valid samples with explicit GSC URL Inspection observations must be `Discovered` or `Indexed` within 24 hours of first sitemap publication. Missing/403/unknown inspection data stays `pending` and is never counted as success.
- **Google indexing gate (primary, delayed):** indexed status is measured separately at T+24–72h and only from explicit GSC `Indexed`/`URL is on Google` evidence. The technical score, sitemap membership, live tests, or rankings do not count; a 90% indexing rate is not claimed until this cohort supplies the denominator and outcome data.

**Pre-live outcome target (user goal):** for at least 90% of valid cohort URLs, an explicit GSC observation captured no later than **one hour before scheduled start (T−1h)** must show both Google discovery (`Discovered` or `Indexed`) and Google indexing (`Indexed`/`URL is on Google`). A page that is only `Discovered – currently not indexed` passes discovery but fails the pre-live indexing goal. This is a measured go/no-go target, not a guarantee Google will meet on every cohort.

**Sampling schedule:** T−48h/T−24h (cohort selection and page contract), T−6h (hub/sitemap recheck), **T−1h (GSC pre-live discovery/index decision)**, match start (same canonical URL becomes live), T+6h (lifecycle handoff), and T+24–72h (post-live GSC follow-up). The denominator is the set of valid exact upcoming candidates selected in the window; no model row is fabricated for a missing opening artifact. The monitor must retain cohort identity across these follow-ups instead of treating each run as a new 12–48-hour sample.
